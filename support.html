Need support?

This page is for students who need a person to talk to — not for science marking.

This app marks GCSE science answers. It cannot help with personal distress, self-harm, or emotional crises, and it does not replace a trusted adult.

If something you typed triggered this page, no marks were awarded for that submission. You can return to the app whenever you are ready.

Talk to someone you trust

  • A teacher, form tutor, pastoral lead, or Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) at school
  • A parent, carer, or another trusted adult in your family
  • A close friend who can help you find adult support

Free support services (UK)

  • Shout — 24/7 Text Support: text SHOUT to 85258 or visit giveusashout.org
  • Childline — confidential support for anyone under 19: call 0800 1111 or visit childline.org.uk
  • Samaritans — 24/7 listening support: call 116 123 or visit samaritans.org
  • NHS 111 — urgent medical or mental health advice: call 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.
