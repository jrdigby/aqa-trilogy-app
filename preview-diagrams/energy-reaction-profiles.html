GCSE chemistry: exothermic and endothermic energy profiles

Reaction coordinate diagrams with activation energy (Ea) and total energy change (ΔH).

Exothermic reaction

  • Products are lower in energy than reactants.
  • Energy is transferred to the surroundings.
  • ΔH is negative.

Endothermic reaction

  • Products are higher in energy than reactants.
  • Energy is taken in from the surroundings.
  • ΔH is positive.
