This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you use our GCSE Science revision platform (the “Service”). We aim to comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

📱 Quick Summary for Students: How We Track Your Data and Progress

  • What we save: Every time you complete a question or type an answer, we record your score, the time you took it, and the specific keywords you got right or missed.
  • Why we track it: We use your past scores to build your practice schedule. If you score highly on a topic, we won't bother you with it for a while. If you struggle or lose marks, we’ll bring that topic back up right when you need to practice it again.
  • Who can see your scores:
    • You: To see your streaks, XP, and topic mastery.
    • Your Teacher: Only if your account is linked to a school class so they can see where you need help.
  • We never sell your question data or use it for adverts.
